# Style: montage (no talking)

The clips carry no speech worth keeping: the order's voice is off unless the brief says
otherwise, and natural sound (sizzle, crunch, pour) stays under the cut.

- Many short shots. Pick each clip's single best moment; most shots hold 0.6-2.0 s.
- Rhythm comes from the cuts, not from music: vary shot sizes (wide, medium, macro) and cut on
  action so the eye is carried from one movement into the next.
- Speed is a tool: speed up waits and repeated motions, keep real speed where the sound sells
  the texture, slow down (60 fps clips only) for the hero texture shot.
- Use labels sparingly: a step name or an amount you can read on screen. The images tell the
  story with the sound off.
- Drop every clip that repeats an angle already used, is soft, or shows nothing changing.
