# Structure: recipe

From ingredients to the finished dish, in true recipe order, so a stranger could follow it with
the sound off.

1. Hook (0-3 s): the finished food in motion (break, pull, pour, slice), with hook text.
2. Process in recipe order. One action per shot, cut mid-action, at most 2 s each. Show what
   changes (melting, combining, rising); skip what does not (an empty bowl, a hand reaching).
   Group repeated actions: one flour drop, not three.
3. Transformation (about 60-75 % in): oven out, flip, sauce thickens; the mini-peak.
4. Payoff: the money shot (inside, first bite, pull), ending where the hook began.
Labels: key step names, and amounts only when you read them on screen or in the note. 4-8 labels.
If a step is missing from the footage, do not fake it: cut around it and name it in
`check_by_eye`.
