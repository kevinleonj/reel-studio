# Structure: showcase

Show the food at its best; the order of events matters less than the rise in appetite.

1. Hook (0-3 s): the most craveable moment in motion (break, pull, pour, drip, steam), with
   hook text that gives a reason to stay.
2. Build: the strongest shots in a rising sequence, each at its peak moment, 1-2 s. Group
   similar shots; never two in a row from the same angle.
3. Peak (about 60-75 % in): the single best texture or reveal.
4. Payoff: the hero shot again or its best variant, ending on a frame that loops back into
   the hook.
