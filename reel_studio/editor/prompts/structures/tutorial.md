# Structure: tutorial

Someone shows and explains how to do one thing. A stranger should be able to do it after one
watch, with the sound on, and follow the steps with the sound off.

1. Hook (0-3 s): the finished result, with the spoken or written promise of what the viewer
   will learn.
2. Steps in true order. Each step is one spoken instruction over the shot that shows it; the
   label names the step in two or three words. Cut the explanation to the instruction itself:
   what to do, and the one detail that matters (heat, time, texture).
3. The tricky step (about 60-75 % in): the one the viewer would get wrong, shown closest and
   longest, with its words kept whole.
4. Result: the finished thing again, ending where the hook began.
If a step is said but not shown, or shown but not said, keep it only if the other half is clear
without it, and name it in `check_by_eye`.
