# Structure: story (one take)

The take already has a beginning, a middle and an end; the edit makes it shorter and sharper
without changing what happened.

1. Hook (0-3 s): the most striking moment of the take, pulled to the front only if the take's
   own opening is slow; the rest stays in order.
2. Middle: the take in order with the slow parts gone. Each kept stretch moves the story: a new
   step, a new reaction, a new line.
3. Turn (about 60-75 % in): the moment the take was filmed for (the reveal, the first taste, the
   result), kept whole with its sound.
4. End: the take's natural ending, or the first frame after the turn that resolves it, cut so
   it loops back into the hook.
